<details>
<summary><b>Zero-shot design of drug-binding proteins via neural iterative selection–expansion</b> — 202606, Nature</summary>

- **Model:** LASErMPNN (Ligand-Aware Sequence Engineering Message-Passing Neural Network)
- **Network Backbone:** Heterograph neural network
- **Pre-training Method:** Pre-training task was to predict atom-level properties
- **Training Data:** Protein–ligand co-crystal structures from the Protein Data Bank (PDB)
- **Downstream Tasks:** Zero-shot de novo design of high-affinity small-molecule binding proteins, side-chain packing and predicting dihedral angles, neural proofreading, and designing proteins to protect labile ligands from hydrolysis

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>AI-predicted spatial transcriptomics unlocks breast cancer biomarkers from pathology</b> — 202605, Cell</summary>

- **Model:** Path2Space
- **Network Backbone:** CTransPath feature extractor + MLP regressor
- **Pre-training Method:** CTransPath self-supervised pathology foundation model features; Path2Space trained by supervised regression on paired H&E-ST data
- **Training Data:** 10x Genomics Visium breast cancer spatial transcriptomics with matched H&E images; Bassiouni et al. cohort, 56,567 matched image-expression spot pairs; predicts 14,068 genes
- **Downstream Tasks:** Spatial gene expression prediction, cell-type abundance inference, TME characterization, SpatioType survival stratification, chemotherapy response prediction, and trastuzumab response prediction

</details>

<summary><b>A deep joint-learning proteomics model for diagnosis of six conditions associated with dementia</b> — 202603, Nat. Med.</summary>

- **Model:** ProtAIDe-Dx
- **Network Backbone:** MLP
- **Pre-training Method:** Utilizes a deep multi-task joint-learning framework trained from scratch using binary cross-entropy and multi-class rank loss
- **Training Data:** Global Neurodegenerative Proteomics Consortium (GNPC) v1.3MS dataset (17,187 participants with SomaLogic 7k proteomics)
- **Downstream Tasks:** Diagnostic prediction of six target conditions (Control, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, Frontotemporal dementia, Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, and Stroke/TIA), and prediction of longitudinal clinical progression

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Genome modelling and design across all domains of life with Evo 2</b> — 202603, Nature</summary>

- **Model:** Evo 2
- **Network Backbone:** StripedHyena 2
- **Pre-training Method:** Two-stage genomic sequence pretraining: 8,192-token pretraining on genic-window-weighted data, followed by multi-stage midtraining/context extension up to 1M tokens
- **Training Data:** OpenGenome2; curated non-redundant nucleotide/DNA sequences from bacteria, archaea, eukarya, and bacteriophage; >8.8T nucleotides; 2.4T tokens for Evo 2 7B and 9.3T tokens for Evo 2 40B; not image data
- **Downstream Tasks:** Variant effect prediction, exon classification, gene essentiality prediction, protein/ncRNA mutational scanning, regulatory element / secondary-structure feature identification, genome-scale generation, and chromatin accessibility controlled generation

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex</b> — 202602, Nature</summary>

- **Model:** Compact DNN models of V4 / Visual Cortex
- **Network Backbone:** ResNet50 (large model); custom compact CNNs (compressed models); also evaluated MobileNet, DenseNet, VGG, Xception, InceptionV4, NASNetMobile, InceptionV3, CORnet
- **Pre-training Method:** Supervised (ImageNet classification) for backbone; then fitted to neural data via closed-loop adaptive experiments
- **Training Data:** Macaque V4, V1, and IT electrophysiology recordings; images from YFCC100M and ImageNet datasets
- **Downstream Tasks:** Predicting neural responses to arbitrary images in macaque visual cortex (V4, V1, IT); stimulus preference characterization; circuit hypothesis generation

</details>
