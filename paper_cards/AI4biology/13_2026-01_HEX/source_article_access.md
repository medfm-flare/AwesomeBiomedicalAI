# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** AI-enabled virtual spatial proteomics from histopathology for interpretable biomarker discovery in lung cancer
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-025-04060-4
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04060-4
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12823406/
- **Author code:** https://github.com/lilab-stanford/HEX
- **Open-access status:** the full text, including a complete Methods section, is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract; the Main section; all named Results subsections ("Study overview", "HEX enables accurate prediction of protein expression from H&E images", "Cross-validation performance", "Independent validation performance", "HEX improves prognosis prediction in early-stage lung cancer", "Biological interpretation of HEX-based prognosis prediction", "HEX improves immunotherapy response prediction in advanced lung cancer", "Biological interpretation of HEX-based immunotherapy response prediction", "Spatial transcriptomics comparison and scaling analyses"); the Discussion; the full Methods including "Study design", the CODEX/PhenoCycler protocol subsections, "CODEX and H&E image registration and dataset construction", "Pan-cancer CODEX–H&E dataset for protein prediction", the cohort descriptions, "WSI processing", "HEX model architecture and training strategy", "HEX model training details", "HEX model evaluation and comparison", "Model architecture for integrating histology and virtual spatial proteomics", "Training and evaluation of MICA", "Biological interpretation of outcome predictions" and "Statistical analysis"; and the complete captions of all six main figures and all ten Extended Data figures.
- **What was not read:** the underlying figure panels themselves (only captions were available in the deposited text); Supplementary Figs. 1–30, which carry the backbone comparison, the FDS and ALF ablations, the PORPOISE late-fusion benchmark, the pan-cancer cross-validation, the virtual-versus-true CODEX equivalence test, the decision-impact and reclassification analyses, the spatial-transcriptomics baselines and the scaling curves; Supplementary Tables 1–4 (antibody panel, cohort clinicopathological characteristics); and the Supplementary Results narrative. Claims resting on those are labelled in the card as described-but-not-directly-assessed. The paper contains no tables in the main text.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
