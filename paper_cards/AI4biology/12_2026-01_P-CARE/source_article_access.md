# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** Genomic risk model to implement precision prostate cancer screening in clinical care: the ProGRESS study
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s43018-025-01103-0
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s43018-025-01103-0
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13181739/
- **Author code:** https://github.com/precimed/MVP-PCa-PHS
- **Trial registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov **NCT05926102** — the Prostate Cancer, Genetic Risk, and Equitable Screening Study (ProGRESS), randomizing 5,000 screen-eligible VA patients to usual care versus P-CARE-informed precision screening. No trial outcomes are reported in this article.
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract; the Results in full, including all five named subsections ("Overview of risk prediction model development", "Association of polygenic score with prostate cancer outcomes", "Association of P-CARE with prostate cancer outcomes", "Development and validation of clinical laboratory assay for genetic prostate cancer risk", "Clinical P-CARE and monogenic reports"); the Discussion; and the **Methods in full**, including study overview, participants and phenotype definitions, the MVP / PRACTICAL / All of Us cohort descriptions, candidate-variant selection and polygenic score training, P-CARE model development and validation, the blended genome-exome assay, analytic and clinical laboratory validation, rare-variant selection and interpretation, clinical report development, and statistics and reproducibility. All three main-figure captions and all four table captions were read via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Supplemental Tables 1–11 (family-history association, the PRACTICAL per-dataset breakdowns, ancestry- and self-reported-race-stratified results, time-dependent AUC, threshold sensitivity/specificity, the random survival forest robustness check, and the risk-equivalent-age table); Supplemental Tables 12–14 (cohort summaries and All of Us ancestry counts); the Supplementary Data file listing the 601 selected variants and their weights; the example clinical laboratory report package in the Supplementary Information; and Extended Data Figs. 1–3, whose captions were read but whose panels were not opened. The underlying table bodies for Tables 1–4 were not available in the deposited text, so every quantitative value cited from a table is one the Results narrative states explicitly. Claims that rest on any of the above are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Data access boundary:** MVP, All of Us and PRACTICAL are all controlled-access resources requiring separate approvals; no participant-level data were accessed for this card, and none of the numbers here were recomputed.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
