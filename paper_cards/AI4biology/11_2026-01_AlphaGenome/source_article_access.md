# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** Advancing regulatory variant effect prediction with AlphaGenome
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10014-0
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12851941/
- **Author code:** https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphagenome — the paper also describes a hosted model and API.
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, Main, all named result sections, the Discussion, and all main-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** the Methods section, which appears only as a stub in the deposited text; Supplementary Tables 1–4 (track metadata and the full per-task benchmark results); Supplementary Figs. 1–12; and Extended Data Figs. 1–9. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
