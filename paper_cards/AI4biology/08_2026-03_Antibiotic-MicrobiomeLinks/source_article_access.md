# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** Antibiotic use and gut microbiome composition links from individual-level prescription data of 14,979 individuals
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-026-04284-y
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04284-y
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13099378/
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, Main, Results and all named result subsections, the Discussion, Tables 1 and 2, and all main-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Methods in full, Supplementary Tables 1–14 (which contain the association catalogue), Supplementary Figures 1–12 and the Extended Data figures. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Data availability boundary:** the study links the Swedish National Prescribed Drug Register to three controlled-access population cohorts (SCAPIS, SIMPLER, MOS). No participant-level data is reproduced or linked here.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
