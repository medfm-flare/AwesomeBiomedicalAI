# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** OMICmAge quantifies biological age by integrating multi-omics with electronic medical records
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s43587-026-01073-7
- **Official route:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-026-01073-7
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13004675/
- **Author code:** https://github.com/LaskySuLab/OMICmAge
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, Main, Results and all named result subsections, the Discussion, and all main-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Methods in full, Supplementary Tables 1–18 (including the EBP correlation tables) and the Extended Data figures. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Data availability boundary:** the study uses four controlled-access human cohorts — Mass General Brigham Biobank, All of Us, TruDiagnostic Biobank and Generation Scotland. No participant-level data is reproduced or linked here.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
