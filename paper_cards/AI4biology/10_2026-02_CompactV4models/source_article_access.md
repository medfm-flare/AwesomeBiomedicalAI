- **Paper:** Compact deep neural network models of the visual cortex
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-026-10150-1
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10150-1
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13359075/
- **Author code:** https://github.com/cowleygroup/V4_compact_models
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, all named result sections, the Discussion, and all main-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Methods (approximately 53,000 characters), Extended Data Figs. 1–9, Supplementary Figures 1–3 and Supplementary Table 2. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Animal research note:** the study reports electrophysiological recordings from three macaques under chronic multi-electrode arrays. Ethical approvals are stated in the article's own declarations, which are the authoritative source; nothing is reproduced here.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
