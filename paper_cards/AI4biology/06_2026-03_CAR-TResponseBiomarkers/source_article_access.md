# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** Predictive biomarkers of response to chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy for pan-haematologic cancer
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41551-026-01633-7
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01633-7
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13435093/
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, Introduction, Results and all named result subsections, the Discussion, Methods, and all main- and Extended-Data-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Supplementary Tables (including Supplementary Table 2, the trial list) and the underlying per-patient data. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Data availability boundary:** the study covers 256 patients enrolled in investigator-initiated clinical trials. No patient-level data is reproduced or linked here.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
