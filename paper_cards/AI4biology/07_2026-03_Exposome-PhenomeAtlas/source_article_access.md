# Source access and rights note

- **Paper:** An atlas of exposome–phenome associations in health and disease risk
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-026-04266-0
- **Official route:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04266-0
- **Open full text:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13099396/
- **Underlying data:** NHANES 1999–2018 is public (https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/); the UK Biobank comparison uses controlled-access data.
- **Open-access status:** the full text is deposited in PubMed Central and was read there.
- **What was read:** the abstract, Main, Results and all named result subsections, the Discussion, and all main-figure captions, via the PMC XML full text.
- **What was not read:** Methods in full, Supplementary Tables 1–9 (which contain the association catalogue itself), Supplementary Figures, Extended Data figures and Extended Data Table 1. Claims that rest on those are labelled in the card as not directly assessed.
- **Rights boundary:** no article PDF and no publisher figure image is reproduced in this folder. The card is a summary and analysis written for this catalogue; it is not a substitute for the Version of Record, and rights in the article remain with the authors and publisher.
